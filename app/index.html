Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, in one playthrough, if you keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, and cheats switch off trophies and saving — keep them off your trophy save. Sources: Output Lag’s launch-week trophy guide; Exophase and PSNProfiles for the official list.
